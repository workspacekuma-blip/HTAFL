# Social Brand System

Use one recognizable system across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and X.

## Primary brand symbol

The **HTAFL Five-Path Mark** is the primary symbol. Its five interconnected paths
represent Hope, Talent, Art, Fashion and Life. Use the mark alone for profile
avatars, the mark + HTAFL wordmark for banners and formal graphics, and the full
`HTAFL` wordmark for editorial branding. Keep Space Grotesk 700, the existing
brand palette and the CREATE / OVERCOME / CONNECT content system.

See [the logo system](logo-system.md) for production SVGs and symbol usage.

## Profile identity

Avatar:
- HTAFL Five-Path Mark alone
- cobalt background + warm-paper mark
- no tiny tagline
- keep generous breathing room

Display name:
**HTAFL**

Bio language should quickly communicate:
- creative resilience
- art/fashion + movement
- community
- CTA

Recommended core bio line:

**Creativity. Resilience. Community. Create. Overcome. Connect.**

Art, fashion, DIY, expression, process.

### 2. OVERCOME

Visual cue:

- high contrast

## Logo placement

Do not stamp a large logo onto every post.

Prefer:
- small mark in a consistent corner
- recognizable layout/colors
- Five-Path Mark + full HTAFL wordmark on banners, campaign openers, formal announcements, and formal graphics
- full `HTAFL` wordmark for editorial branding

## Instagram

Prioritize:
- Reels
- carousels
- Stories
- community showcases

Carousel behavior:
- slide 1 = strong hook
- middle = useful content
- final = clear CTA

## TikTok

Keep covers simple:
- 3–6 word title
- high contrast
- recognizable category cue
- avoid over-designed thumbnails

Video should feel human and native rather than like a television commercial.

## Facebook

Allow:
- longer story copy
- events
- community updates
- partner/collaboration posts

Use the same visual identity without forcing Instagram layouts into every Facebook post.

## X / Twitter

Use:
- clean quote cards
- data/stat cards only when sources are verified
- conversation prompts
- thread openers
- event/campaign graphics

## Campaign language

Use the three-word system flexibly:

## Working canvases

These are reusable HTAFL production presets, not claims about every organic/ad
placement's current limits. Adapt the composition rather than stretching artwork.

| Preset | Canvas | Primary use | Internal spacing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Portrait | 1080 × 1350, 4:5 | Instagram feed/carousels; Facebook feed | 80px sides; 76px footer margin |
| Square | 1080 × 1080, 1:1 | Shared feed cards and square carousel variants | 80px sides; retain readable copy |
| Vertical | 1080 × 1920, 9:16 | Instagram/Facebook Reels and Stories; TikTok covers | Important content inside x=88–840, y=340–1520 |
| Landscape | 1200 × 675, 16:9 | X posts/thread openers; Facebook landscape variants | 64px sides; split image/text when needed |
| Avatar | 1024 × 1024 master | All four profile identities | Centered 640px mark viewport; generous circular-crop clearance |
| Formal banner | 1500 × 500 | X header; source composition for other actual banner placements | Centered mark + full HTAFL; no corner-critical copy |

The vertical box is an HTAFL conservative design guide, not an official universal
safe zone. It leaves 240px on the right and 400px below important material. Keep
cover titles near the center, outside imagery and within this box. Check each
app's feed, full-screen and profile-grid previews; UI, captions and crops vary.
Decorative background may extend to the canvas edge. Banner composition is not
an Instagram/TikTok profile-header feature or a universal Facebook cover size.

TikTok's official ad guidance recommends vertical 9:16 and explains that safe
zones depend on dimensions, caption length and additional formats; these ad rules
are not organic-cover requirements. See [TikTok specifications](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads?lang=en-GB).
X recommends a 1500 × 500 header and 400 × 400 profile photo; downsample the master
avatar for that placement. See [X profile guidance](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/how-to-customize-your-profile).
Meta's current feed guides require login in this environment; portrait/square
presets above are our working formats, not independently verified Meta limits.
Check [Instagram feed](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/instagram-feed)
and [Facebook feed](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/facebook-feed)
in the actual placement preview before publishing. Platform guidance checked 5 October 2026.

## Platform adaptations

| Channel | Use the pack for | Copy and participation behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram | Portrait carousel hook → useful middle slides → final CTA; square posts; vertical Reel/Story covers; approved showcases | One clear action per post. Keep the carousel's family, format, page numbering and campaign branding consistent. Put credits/sources in the caption and supply alt text for each slide. |
| TikTok | Vertical cover with 3–6 words, one visible CREATE/OVERCOME/CONNECT cue and a small mark | Human process, real voice and practical expression. The cover supports the video; it does not become a dense slide. Caption actual speech, retain the right/lower UI clearance and verify the profile crop. |
| Facebook | Portrait or landscape events, community updates, founder notes, collaboration posts and educational albums | Allow a fuller story in the post text. Keep visual copy short; confirm date/time zone, venue, access arrangements and the actual participation destination. Adapt photos to the composition instead of blindly reposting a carousel. |
| X | Landscape or square quote cards, practical points, conversation prompts, thread openers and campaign/event graphics | One idea per graphic; details and source links belong in the accompanying post/thread. Use data/stat cards only with a verified source and date. Use the full wordmark for editorial graphics. |

Use the core bio line above where it fits the channel. A shorter option is
**Art. Fashion. Resilience. Create. Overcome. Connect.** Add only a real destination
and a truthful participation CTA. Do not invent handles, live signup availability
or a publishing schedule.

## Reusable visual components

| Component | Shared rule |
| --- | --- |
| Family label | Always display CREATE, OVERCOME or CONNECT as text. Color alone must not convey the family. Inter 500, clear reading order. |
| Headline | Space Grotesk 700; short, 1–3 lines. Landscape uses 60px; square 78px; portrait/vertical 88px in these master canvases. |
| Supporting copy | Inter 400; Inter 500 for CTA/labels. 36px in large masters, 28px in landscape. Split long copy into slides; do not squeeze paragraphs into covers. |
| Image frame | One approved image or a purposeful process/collage composition. Never put paragraph copy over photos. No decorative fake people or submissions. |
| Step rows | 01/02/03 badges plus a practical phrase; up to three ideas per slide. Numbering is an aid, not a replacement for the instruction. |
| Connected line | Static, thin cobalt link for CONNECT. It supports real relationships between ideas; no constant animation. |
| CTA | One honest next step: try, reflect, save, discuss or visit a real destination. No fake buttons, deadlines or clickable-looking controls inside the artwork. |
| Editorial brand | Full HTAFL wordmark alone for quotes, founder notes and instructional editorial work. |
| Campaign/formal brand | Small Five-Path Mark + full HTAFL wordmark on openers, awareness campaigns and confirmed events. |
| Regular image/video brand | Small Five-Path Mark in a consistent top corner; avoid a dominant stamp on every image. |

CREATE uses cobalt with white headlines and paper image/process frames. OVERCOME
uses paper with ink, a coral accent and step hierarchy. CONNECT uses blue with
warm neutrals, the connection line and consent-approved photography where relevant.
Quotes and instructional CONNECT posts can remain type-led; do not add a photo
just to fill a frame. Never add another palette or change individual logo path colors.
Use ink on paper/mist/coral and white on cobalt; do not use white body text on coral.

## Template recipes

All layouts can use any of the three families through the generator. Defaults
express the subject; they are not additional content pillars.

| Template / file id | Default family | Editable slots and composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quote / `quote` | OVERCOME | Oversized approved statement, verified attribution/permission, one relevant next step. An original statement may omit attribution; never assign a quote to someone without verification. |
| Educational carousel / `carousel-hook`, `carousel-content`, `carousel-cta` | OVERCOME | Hook/question and benefit → one idea with up to three practical points → recap and single CTA. Duplicate the middle slide as needed and update all numbering. |
| Practical tip / `practical-tip` | OVERCOME | One action, how to begin, optional reflection, gentle invitation. Avoid diagnosis, treatment or exaggerated outcome claims. |
| Challenge launch / `challenge-launch` | CREATE | Actual prompt, confirmed timing or explicit own-pace framing, real participation route, honest CTA. Full campaign lockup on opener. |
| Challenge reminder / `challenge-reminder` | CONNECT | One small way to participate, actual status/deadline if present, same campaign family and real destination. Do not invent urgency. |
| Community spotlight / `community-spotlight` | CONNECT | Approved title, real image, participant-chosen public credit, approved invitation. If none exists, use an invitation graphic with no image/person profile. |
| Founder note / `founder-note` | OVERCOME | Founder-approved headline, two brief personal reflections, approved signature. Keep the founder's meaning and voice; never manufacture a founder statement. |
| Awareness / `awareness` | OVERCOME | Clear topic, verified context, practical non-clinical action, source/date and one supportive CTA. No unsourced numbers or sensational language. |
| Event / `event` | CONNECT | Confirmed event name, date/time/time zone, venue/access details, real registration/contact destination and one CTA. No fabricated events. |
| Creative showcase / `creative-showcase` | CREATE | Approved artwork/process photo, approved work title and creator credit, material/process note. Respect image rights and participant consent. |
| Weekly recap / `weekly-recap` | CONNECT | Verified activity, contribution and confirmed next opportunity. No invented reach, impact or community statistics. |
| Video/Reel cover / `video-cover` | CREATE | 3–6 word title, one category cue, approved image or intentional type-led replacement. Center key material, clear lower/right zones, no paragraphs. |

The empty frames are editing placeholders, not publication-ready community posts.
For a finished invitation with no approved image, change `layout` to `editorial`,
replace the bracketed text with a truthful invitation and remove unsupported credits.

## Caption, access and approval

Caption order: clear opening → useful context → one action → actual credits/source
links → a small selection of the existing hashtags. Use the established human,
practical and encouraging voice; leave room for choice, beginners and different
abilities. The CREATE / OVERCOME / CONNECT campaign language above is unchanged.

Before publication, replace all placeholders, confirm facts/destinations and obtain
permission for identifiable people, stories, quotations and creative work. Keep
consent records private. Supply descriptive platform alt text and accurate video
captions; meaningful words must also be available outside the image. Avoid rapid
flashing, forced movement or audio-dependent meaning. Static cover art requires
no animation or reduced-motion interaction.

The SVGs are editable sources. Use the exact fonts or outline them, then export
PNG/JPEG at the intended canvas size. Review small-screen readability, circular
avatar cropping, title/mark clearance, family label, source/credit and actual UI
overlays. Do not publish directly from a placeholder preview.
