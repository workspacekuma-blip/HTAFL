# Privacy operations — Nigeria

Owner-confirmed public contact: **Operations Manager**, Nigeria,
`htafl@africamail.com`. The **HTAFL owner** reviews community uploads.
`content/privacy-policy.json` is the central source rendered by the existing
page generator into `/privacy/`. The supplied operator label is a role; it does
not establish an incorporated entity or registration.

The notice uses purpose-based retention, not a fabricated legal number of days.
Review pending work regularly. Delete declined/withdrawn work through the review
tools; end abandoned review rather than retaining submissions indefinitely.
Keep published work only while permission and purpose remain. Delete enquiry
emails when handling/follow-up ends unless another documented lawful basis
requires retention. Separately remove inbox attachments, exports and backup copies.
These are owner procedures; no automatic cleanup/backup workflow is claimed.

Handle access/correction/deletion and consent withdrawal through the named inbox.
Do not publish private emails or review notes. Obtain and appropriately verify
guardian permission before accepting children's personal information or images;
the current online forms do not implement a verified guardian workflow. A policy
paragraph or ordinary consent checkbox is not proof of guardian verification.
Complete this operational safeguard before inviting children to submit directly.

## Review procedure — assigned to the HTAFL owner

1. Sign in through `/admin/` on the deployed HTTPS site. Keep the passphrase in
   a password manager, never in this repository or a shared message. Sign out
   when finished; use a private, updated device for review.
2. Leave new work pending and private. Check the rights and contact permission,
   the actual work, creator credit and description. Do not treat a checked box
   as evidence that another person's image, a child's data or a third-party
   artwork can be published.
3. If ownership or permission is unclear, contact the submitter through the
   HTAFL inbox and resolve it before approval. Do not request identity documents,
   health histories or other sensitive information as a routine requirement.
4. For children, arrange and appropriately verify guardian involvement before
   accepting their personal information. Until that process is in place, direct
   the guardian to the Operations Manager and do not invite direct child uploads.
5. Approve only when optional publication permission is present and all checks
   are satisfied. Approval exposes the work and public credit, never the email
   or review notes. Work without publication permission must remain private.
6. Reject work that cannot be accepted, or withdraw published work when permission
   is withdrawn. These actions delete the application record and image; also
   remove related email attachments and any separately held copies.

## Weekly retention review and rights requests

The owner must carry out this review; it is not an automated or scheduled service.

- Check pending submissions and enquiry threads. Resolve active items and close
  abandoned review. Do not keep declined or unneeded work indefinitely.
- Check that published work still has permission and a current use. Withdraw it
  when the purpose ends, or promptly when the creator withdraws permission.
- Record access/correction/deletion requests in a protected private ledger with
  received date, submission reference, action, follow-up and completion date.
  Do not store this ledger in the public site or Git repository.
- Verify a request proportionately, preferably through the original submission
  email and reference. Do not disclose records based on a reference alone.
- For deletion, remove the website record and image, inbox attachments, exports
  and backup copies. Confirm completion with the requester and document any
  specific reason a record must lawfully be retained.
- Check the private storage for interrupted deletions and the access-state store
  for expired objects. Sessions expire on access; physical object housekeeping
  and recovery backups still require an operator process.

## Before opening public submissions

Confirm the actual controller identity, guardian verification procedure, provider
processing arrangements and a workable deletion/recovery process. Verify on the
live host that a pending upload cannot be retrieved publicly, survives a redeploy,
and disappears after withdrawal. Keep only the test result in this repository,
not the test submitter's personal data, credentials or private artwork.

Review Netlify/mail.com/Gmail terms, actual processing locations and safeguards for any
cross-border transfers. Confirm the legal controller's identity if different
from the owner-provided operator label. This implementation is not a legal
compliance certification or evidence of regulator registration.
