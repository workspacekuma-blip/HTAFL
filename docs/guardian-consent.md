# Guardian consent procedure — Operations Manager

Owner/reviewer: HTAFL owner. Contact: htafl@africamail.com. Country: Nigeria.
Prepared 7 October 2026. This procedure is an operational safeguard, not evidence
that a guardian has already been verified or a legal compliance certification.

## Current online boundary

All five involvement forms and the community upload form require an adult-only
confirmation, enforced by the server before sending email or saving a submission.
No date of birth, identity document or health history is requested. This is a
self-declaration, not age verification. Reviewers must check the actual content.
Children must not submit personal details or identifiable images through these
forms. A guardian contacts the Operations Manager first, initially without child
details or images. Do not ask a child to tick the adult confirmation.

## Before accepting any child's information

1. Respond privately to the adult's initial enquiry. Explain the proposed
   activity, exactly what data is needed, who can see it, the retention purpose,
   provider processing and how to withdraw. Provide the current Privacy Notice.
   If guardian verification cannot be arranged, do not collect the child's data.
2. Establish that the contact is an adult with legal guardian authority using a
   proportionate, documented method appropriate to the case. A checkbox, shared
   surname or email address alone is insufficient. Use a private conversation
   and an appropriate authority check; resolve uncertainty before proceeding.
   Do not routinely collect ID copies or retain identity documents. If additional
   proof is necessary, establish secure handling and deletion before requesting it.
3. Obtain specific permission for the activity and necessary private processing.
   Seek the child's age-appropriate understanding and assent. Do not proceed
   against a child's objection. Keep health information and unnecessary school,
   location or family details out of the submission.
4. Ask separately about publication: the exact work, credit (prefer a chosen
   non-identifying credit), caption, image and intended public channels. Refusing
   publication must not prevent private participation. Obtain permission from
   every identifiable person and rights holder; avoid identifiable child images
   where an image of the artwork alone will suffice.
5. Record the decision in a protected consent ledger, outside Git/public folders:
   internal reference; notice/consent version; purpose; guardian contact;
   relationship/authority confirmed; verification method and date; reviewer;
   processing permission; separate publication scope/choice; child's assent;
   follow-up/review trigger; withdrawal and deletion dates. Record the outcome,
   not copies of verification documents. Never put this ledger in public captions.
6. Agree a secure private collection method only after these steps. The current
   online form is deliberately not a verified guardian-submission portal. Do not
   bypass its adult-only boundary. Do not approve a child's submission using only
   an ordinary online consent record.

## Unexpected child data, withdrawal and recovery

Leave suspect work private. Do not approve or forward it. Contact the adult only
as necessary to resolve safeguarding and delete information that cannot lawfully
be accepted. The HTAFL owner handles review; the Operations Manager handles the
privacy request and records the outcome privately.

On withdrawal, remove the website record and image, inbox attachments and private
exports. Delete or replace every recovery archive containing that reference,
including copies on mail.com Drive. Keep a minimum private deletion ledger so a
recovery cannot resurrect withdrawn work. Recovered work is pending with public
permission disabled; obtain fresh permission and review before any republication.

Review permission when the purpose or public channels change, when the child
reaches adulthood, or on a request. Do not invent a universal legal retention term.

Primary basis checked 7 October 2026: section 31 of the
[Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)
requires guardian consent when relying on consent for a child and appropriate
age/consent verification. The
[NDPC final 2025 implementation directive](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf)
is the accompanying official reference. The adult-only boundary is HTAFL's
implementation choice, not a claim that the law prescribes this checkbox.
