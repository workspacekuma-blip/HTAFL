# YouTube Brand System

YouTube should feel like the long-form home of the HTAFL brand.

## Primary brand symbol

Use the **HTAFL Five-Path Mark** alone for the channel avatar, the mark + HTAFL
wordmark for banners and formal graphics, and the full `HTAFL` wordmark for
editorial branding such as chapter/title cards and quote or reference cards.
Keep the existing Space Grotesk typography, palette and CREATE / OVERCOME /
CONNECT content architecture.

See [the logo system](logo-system.md) for the mark meaning and production SVGs.

## Channel identity

Avatar:
- HTAFL Five-Path Mark alone
- cobalt background
- warm-paper symbol

Banner:
- Five-Path Mark + full `HTAFL` wordmark lockup
- tagline: `CREATE. OVERCOME. CONNECT.`
- layered creative imagery or graphic motif
- keep essential information in the center-safe area
- avoid clutter

Channel description should explain:
- mission
- creative expression
- resilience
- movement
- community
- what viewers can expect

## Thumbnail system

Every thumbnail should be recognizable before the logo is noticed.

Rules:
- 3–5 words preferred
- one strong focal image
- large Space Grotesk headline
- high contrast
- use cobalt / warm paper / ink as base
- coral only as accent
- HTAFL Five-Path Mark small, consistent position
- never use misleading shock expressions
- never sensationalize mental-health subjects

Category cue:
- CREATE = cobalt band
- OVERCOME = coral detail on warm-paper/ink composition
- CONNECT = ink + photography + cobalt connection line

## Video package

Create reusable:

- 2–3 second brand sting maximum
- lower-third
- chapter/title card
- quote card

- subscribe/end card
- Shorts title treatment

Intro should not delay the content.

## Shorts

Visual behavior:
- captions always
- safe margins
- strong first-frame title when needed
- native pacing
- minimal logo presence
- clear CTA only when useful

## Accessibility

- accurate captions
- readable on-screen text
- sufficient contrast
- describe important visual information when necessary
- links/resources in description where appropriate

## Mental-health content

Never:
- sensationalize crisis
- imply HTAFL replaces professional care
- publish unverified advice
- use thumbnails that exploit distress

When professional/crisis resources are relevant, use geographically appropriate, verified information.

YouTube recommends 2560×1440 banners, minimum 2048×1152, and a 1235×338 text/logo
safe area at that minimum size. Our guide deliberately keeps a conservative
1235×338 rectangle centered on the **larger** canvas (x=662.5, y=551), rather than
claiming it is the platform's scaled safe-area dimension. Export a banner at 6MB
or less and confirm mobile/desktop/TV crops. Profile art should remain legible at
98×98 display size. [Official channel branding guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en).

Current guidance recommends 3840×2160 video and 2160×3840 Shorts thumbnails in
JPG/PNG, at 16:9 and 9:16 respectively. Upload limits differ between desktop and
mobile; verify in Studio. YouTube currently supports custom Shorts thumbnails
through desktop Studio for verified accounts, so these are upload-ready design
canvases after rasterization and content approval, not a guarantee of account
eligibility. [Official thumbnail guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en).
The Shorts safe box above is an HTAFL internal clearance guide; app UI and crops
still need previewing. Guidance checked 5 October 2026.

## Thumbnail and cover composition

Keep the same family label, corner mark and typography across a series. Titles
are Space Grotesk 700; supporting copy and caption treatments are Inter 400/500.
Use one focal image: an approved process, work, interview subject or relevant
scene. An empty frame is an editing placeholder, not a fake photograph. If no
image is available, recompose a truthful type-led card rather than manufacture
a person or emotional reaction.

Leave the thumbnail's lower-right corner clear for the duration badge. The
headline and image are separate; no paragraphs over photography. Check the title
at small feed size. Coral stays an accent, never a competing full thumbnail palette.
No sensational crisis framing, distress thumbnails, misleading before/after
claims, unsourced health claims or promises that HTAFL replaces professional care.

Editorial graphics use the full HTAFL wordmark; the avatar is mark-only; banner
and formal sting use the mark + full wordmark. Do not recolor separate paths or
recreate the star geometry. The lower third uses the mark only at small scale.

For a full-HD lower third, use a short ~52px name and ~32px role; for chapter cards
use ~120px headlines and ~44px support. Quote headlines use ~112px, source titles
~88px and source details ~40px. These are production-canvas values, not CSS page
type scales. After editing, inspect actual font metrics and small-screen legibility.

## End-screen placement and timing

Use the supplied clean background with **Keep creating. Keep moving. Stay connected.**
The separate guide reserves two 650×366 video/playlist regions at (120,450) and
(850,450), plus a subscribe circle centered at (1710,633). Adjust these illustrative
positions to native Studio element constraints and the real viewer preview.
Add actual Next video, Playlist and Subscribe elements; SVG shapes do not become
links or enrollment controls.

Native end screens run in the final 5–20 seconds, require a video at least 25 seconds
long, and are unavailable on made-for-kids videos. Keep a spoken/description route
to the relevant content where native elements are unavailable. Preview the actual
elements instead of assuming the guide is a live interface.
[Official end-screen guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en).

## Short brand sting

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0–800ms | Five geometric paths reveal sequentially, 160ms each, using the website's directional clipping approach |
| 800–960ms | Full HTAFL wordmark fades in |
| 960–1120ms | Create. Overcome. Connect. fades in |
| 1120–2400ms | Hold the exact static lockup, then cut to content |

Maximum package length is 3 seconds; the default is 2.4 seconds. Use after the
opening hook or as a brief transition. The preview is silent, manual and one-shot;
no bouncing, glow, rotation, repeated motion or sound. It cancels on a live
reduced-motion preference and shows a static lockup. Rendered video cannot detect
that preference; produce a static/direct-cut alternative where needed. The pack
contains the design and timing template, not a rendered MP4 or soundtrack.

Provide accurate captions and meaningful spoken visual descriptions. Put real
sources and appropriate resources in the description when relevant; verify any
professional/crisis resource for the intended geography at publication time.
