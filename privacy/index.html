Privacy

Your work. Your choices.

Who handles your information

The Operations Manager is HTAFL’s contact for this website and its community submissions in Nigeria. The HTAFL owner reviews community uploads. For privacy questions, contact htafl@africamail.com.

Contact enquiries

We use your name, email, message, response consent and the preferences relevant to your chosen pathway to respond to your enquiry. These may include creative interests, skills, availability or an optional organisation or portfolio link. Messages go to htafl@africamail.com through the configured email delivery service. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Financial support

The Support page displays public recipient bank details and, when supplied, a public crypto wallet address and network. Transfers happen through your bank or wallet service under its own privacy policy. This website does not collect your banking or wallet credentials, process transfers, verify payment or store payment records. Do not include private financial information in an enquiry.

Creative submissions

We store uploads privately for review with their title, creator credit, description, image description, email and consent choices. We re-encode images and remove embedded metadata. Your contact email and review records are excluded from the public gallery. Only submit work you own or have permission to share, with permission from people shown.

Your consent and public sharing

Your response and review consent lets HTAFL handle your enquiry or submission for that purpose. Publication permission is a separate, optional choice. Approved work can appear with its title, creator credit, description and image description. We do not publish contact email addresses. You can withdraw permission by contacting htafl@africamail.com with the submission reference.

Retention

We keep information only while needed for its stated purpose. Pending work is kept while review is active. Rejected or withdrawn work is removed from the website’s upload store. Approved work is kept while publication permission remains active and HTAFL continues to use it. Enquiry emails are kept only as needed to handle the enquiry and any agreed follow-up. Inbox and backup copies need separate removal by the Operations Manager. There is no universal fixed retention period for all these records.

Access, correction and deletion

Contact htafl@africamail.com to request access to, correction of or deletion of your information, or to withdraw consent. Include your submission reference where available. HTAFL may ask for proportionate information to confirm a request belongs to you. You can also raise a concern with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children and guardian involvement

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to contact the Operations Manager before sending personal information or identifiable work. HTAFL must arrange appropriate guardian consent and verification before accepting a child’s submission. Do not upload a child’s image or personal information without the required permission.

Service providers

The selected public hosting service is Netlify, including private object storage for uploads when deployed. Mail.com hosts the HTAFL africamail.com inbox. Gmail sends configured enquiries and submission notifications to that inbox. These providers may process information outside Nigeria under their own service and privacy terms. The local preview uses private server storage. We do not sell submissions or contact information.

Cookies and storage

The public website has no visitor accounts, analytics or advertising cookies. Resource filters use the page URL. The private review dashboard uses an essential sign-in cookie and protected server-side session records. Hosting and email providers may keep operational logs under their own policies.

Updated 7 October 2026. This notice follows the storage-limitation principles of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (opens in a new tab) .
