Overcome / Resilience

Move. Reflect.
Reset. Try again.

Explore small, adaptable practices at your own pace. Participation can be quiet, private and entirely your own.

At your own pace

Make room for a small beginning.

  • Movement

    Choose a comfortable way to move, pause or spend time outdoors. Adapt it to your abilities and circumstances.

  • Reflection

    Use writing, drawing or a quiet moment to notice your own experience. You choose what stays private.

  • Resilience

    Trying again can be a small action. Rest, a new approach or reaching out can also be part of it.
